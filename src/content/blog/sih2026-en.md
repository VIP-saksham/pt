Smart India Hackathon is a different kind of engineering exercise. The problem statements are real, the time is short, and the demo has to survive judges who click things you didn't expect.

Our team got there with **AICTE Compliance OS** — a platform that helps institutes handle AICTE compliance workflows without drowning in spreadsheets and reminders. Making it grand finals took three things we kept repeating:

1. **Scope brutally.** One workflow done well beats five workflows half-built.
2. **Demo like a user, not a dev.** Every screen one click from value.
3. **Backend that doesn't flinch.** If the judging machine internet dies, the demo still runs.

Where it goes next: packaging, more institutes, and making the compliance experience boring — in the best possible way, where nobody has to think about whether the paperwork is done right.
