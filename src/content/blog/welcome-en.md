Hi! I'm **Saksham Swaroop** — a student developer from Delhi, India, currently doing my **B.Tech at MAIT**.

This site is where everything I build lands. A quick tour of what you'll find here:

- **AICTE Compliance OS** — my Smart India Hackathon 2026 grand-finalist project: a compliance platform that helps institutes stay aligned with AICTE norms.
- **M2M — Mitti to Market** — a full-stack agricultural marketplace where farmers get AI-assisted crop assessment (OpenCV signals, optional PyTorch preprocessing) and SELL / STORE / PROCESS decisions.
- **Telegram Music Bots** — KeyaraMusic and friends: playback, streaming, queues, automation and database-backed settings.
- **NikoPad** — a text & code editor prototype engineered directly into the Notepad++ 8.9.8 x64 binary via PE resource modification.
- **NEXHOST / BotCloud** — my VPS hosting and deployment platform, in active private development.

I like building systems end to end — the API, the database, the bot, the deploy pipeline. If it automates something boring or makes a product feel real, I'm in.
